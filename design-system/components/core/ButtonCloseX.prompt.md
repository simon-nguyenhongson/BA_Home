The ✕ dismiss control for modals, drawers and callouts — 3 sizes, gray or primary, light or dark theme.

theme="dark" is for placing on an inverse surface.

```jsx
<ButtonCloseX />
```

Props
- `size` — `sm` | `md` | `lg`
- `color` — `gray` | `primary`
- `theme` — `light` | `dark`
- `state` — `default` | `hover` | `focused`
