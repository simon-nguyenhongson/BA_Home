Avatar plus name and a secondary line (email, role), 4 sizes.

The standard person row for menus, tables and assignee pickers.

```jsx
<AvatarLabelGroup />
```

Props
- `size` — `sm` | `md` | `lg` | `xl`
- `statusIcon` — `online indicator` | `false` | `company` | `verified`
- `state` — `default` | `hover` | `focus`
