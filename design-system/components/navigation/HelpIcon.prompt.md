The ⓘ help affordance that opens a tooltip.

Place after a label, never in place of one.

```jsx
<HelpIcon />
```

Props
- `cursor` — boolean
- `open` — boolean
- `supportingText` — `false`
- `tooltip` — `bottom` | `left` | `right` | `top arrow` | `top left` | `top right` | `bottom left` | `bottom right`
